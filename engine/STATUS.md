## Layout

The repository's folders (PROJ-510, journal 87): `engine/` is this workspace (`crates/`, `fixtures/`, `packaging/`,
this file, PLAN and JOURNAL) with `engine/runtime/` (the runtime Markdown, which FERB.app carries as
`Contents/Resources/ferb`; the migrations, compiled into ferbd; the secret helper's source); `apps/mac` is FERB.app (`FERBNotch`, `FERBAgent`),
`apps/android` and `apps/ios` wait for stages 3 and 4; `extension/` is the unpacked browser extension (FERB.app's
`Contents/Resources/extensions/chromium`) and `extension-store/` its store listing. Before 2026-11-16 these were
`rust/`, `ferb/`, `ui/`, `extensions/chromium` and `extensions/store`: older journal entries and the Python column
below use those names.

One line per crate. **Portable** crates build for iOS and Android (`engine/gate.sh portable`) and are the foundation
the phone apps share; they reach the Mac's own parts only through traits.

| Crate | What it is | Builds for |
|---|---|---|
