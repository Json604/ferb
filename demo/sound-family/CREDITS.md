# Sound credits

## First launch sound

`source/google-material-hero.mp4` and the file rendered from it (`out/first-launch/ferb-first-launch.wav`) use a
"hero" sound from Google's Material Design sound resources, by Google, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for FERB: leading silence trimmed, leveled to −24 LUFS. Nothing else was changed.

Licence status: CC BY 4.0, confirmed by the owner (2026-11-01); also stated by a project that redistributes these
files (https://github.com/nana-4/materia-sound-theme). CC BY needs this credit to ship with FERB (for example in the
app's About or acknowledgements).

## Notification sound

`source/material/notification_high-intensity.ogg` and the files rendered from it (`ferb-notification-*.wav`) are from
the same Google Material Design sound resources (CC BY 4.0, by Google), taken from the redistribution at
https://github.com/nana-4/materia-sound-theme. Changes: trimmed, leveled, a small room added; the Subtle version is also
low-passed at 3 kHz.

## Everything else

All other sounds are synthesised from code in `render.py` for FERB.
