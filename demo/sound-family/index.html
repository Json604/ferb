FERB Sound Family

The decided family. FERB opens with the Material clip, played once, then silence until the Crystal logo as the notch says hello. Everyday cues are Crystal glass: bright for things that went well (summon, listening, done), damped for paused and error, and a single ringing A for needs you. Every notification (banners, reminders, schedules, notices in the notch) plays the Material notification sound. The orb plays drip-off when it sets off, melt-back when it arrives, and the hop.

This page is open from disk, so only single sounds play. For the tour, run python3 -m http.server -d demo/sound-family and open http://localhost:8000.

    How these were made, and the files
    

      
Source
demo/sound-family/render.py. Every sound is synthesised from code, with no samples. Re-rendering regenerates every variant, intensity and stem.

      
Two voices, one glass
Bright: a ringing note over a warm body an octave down, a strike that is gone within 40 ms, a slow shimmer. Damped: the same body and note ringing for about a third of a second, glassy overtones for only a few tens of milliseconds, a softer contact and no strike.

      
Per sound
Dry mono (dry/), room stem (wet/), stereo reference (ref/), and the room as an impulse response. 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV. Option files carry -o1, -o2 or -o3 until you pick.

      
Checks (all pass)
Loudness on target · true peak ≤ −1 dBTP · starts on its first sample · ends on exact zero · ≤ 2% energy below 200 Hz · ≥ 85% in 250 Hz–4 kHz · < 3% above 8 kHz.

      
Notification
Google's Material Design sound resources (notification_high-intensity), CC BY 4.0. Changed: trimmed, leveled, a small room added; Subtle is also darker.

      
First launch
From Google's Material Design sound resources ("hero" sound), licensed CC BY 4.0; credited in demo/sound-family/CREDITS.md. Changed: silence trimmed, leveled to −24 LUFS; nothing else.

      
Levels
Logo −20 · Needs you −23 (1 dB lower than before) · Done, Error −26 · Summon −28 · Listening, Paused −30 · Orb −32 to −36 LUFS. Subtle is 5 dB quieter, shorter and darker.
