A personal assistant for your Mac. Give it something to do, keep doing your thing, and it takes care of the steps in between.

what it does

  • Works across your Mac, quietly in the background. Your cursor, keyboard and focus stay yours.
  • Remembers your preferences and gets better at work you repeat. You can review, correct or remove what it remembers.
  • Reminders and scheduled work arrive from the notch.
  • Prepares hard-to-undo actions and asks you first. You can stop any task.
  • Works with the AI you choose: ChatGPT, Claude or an API key.

your data

Your tasks, history and memory are stored on your Mac; there is no FERB server. To do its work, FERB sends context to the AI service you connect, with known kinds of secrets removed first. Passwords and one-time codes are filled through a sealed path: the model gets a handle, never the secret.

Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 14.2 or later

  1. Open the DMG and drag FERB to Applications.
  2. Connect your AI and allow its permissions.
  3. Start with one thing: ask FERB to lend a hand.
Opening FERB for the first time

The current build uses FERB’s own signing certificate. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway for FERB. Then open FERB from Applications.
